Scientific discovery of geothermal-indicating faults — grounded in official, verified sources. Mirrors knowledge/ in the repo.

Why faults = geothermal

  • Faults are crustal fractures with measurable displacement; their surface intersections are fault traces; clusters of related fractures are fault zones (competition About page).
  • Fractured crust provides fluid pathways: “faults … act as conduits for fluids, concentrating minerals, hydrocarbons, geothermal energy, and groundwater” (About page). The Steamboat geothermal plants near Reno sit inside the GeoDAWN study area (About page figure caption).
  • Detecting faults uses field evidence (scarps, offsets, slickensides), seismic reflection, gravity/magnetic anomalies, remote sensing & elevation data, and computational methods — “edge detection, Hough transforms, and deep learning” (About page).

Region: Walker Lane & western Great Basin

GeoDAWN (Geoscience Data Acquisition for Western Nevada) spans “areas of major resource potential associated with the Walker Lane and western Great Basin” — 149,030 line-km over 51,857 km² flown Nov 2021–Nov 2022 under USGS EarthMRI with DOE Geothermal Technologies Office support. Area 2 (most of the extent) was flown “primarily with a focus on geothermal resources”. Source: USGS data release, DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ (CC0), landing page usgs.gov.

Most faults in the region “are more subtle, and many are hidden below the surface, requiring geophysical data to detect” (competition About page) — which is exactly why the scored faults are absent from the surface-compiled USGS catalogue.

Where unmapped faults are expected (working hypotheses)

  1. Under basin fill: range-front normal faults continue beneath alluvium; magnetic/gravity gradients and basement-depth contrast (feature band 15) expose them.
  2. Along-strike extensions of mapped strands (systematic under-mapping of fault tips).
  3. Splays & stepovers of known systems — the experts re-examined precisely these data.
  4. Geodetically active, geologically unmapped: strain-rate bands (4, 7, 8) and seismicity bands (10, 16) flag structures with no catalogue trace; cf. Kreemer & Young 2022 on western-US crustal strain.
  5. Lineaments in derivative fields: tilt/total curvature (band 6), TMI horizontal gradient (band 3), isostatic gravity horizontal gradient (band 18) are edge-detector products; coherent linear features ≥ ~2 km off-catalogue are expert-style discoveries.
