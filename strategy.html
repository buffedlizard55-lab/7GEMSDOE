Contrarian where it pays, measured everywhere. The scored population is unmapped faults — the strategy is built from that fact.

The asymmetry that defines everything

  • Scoring population ≠ training labels. Both prize rounds use expert-labelled faults absent from the USGS catalogue (rules, verbatim in Research). Catalogue DTI is a plumbing monitor only.
  • β/α = 4. Misses cost 4× false positives; the 300 m kernel gives partial credit for near misses. Mass placed near real structure is cheap; mass in dead ground is waste.
  • Final round rewards discovery. Experts mine every submission for plausible new faults; verified discoveries expand the label set and re-score us. Credible off-catalogue candidates are an investment in the $250K round, not just the $50K round.
  • Budget: 3 submissions/week/account. The five team accounts give 15 experiments/week; allocate them across distinct hypotheses, keep one control.

Contrarian finding: against hidden faults the DTI rises with halo width up to ~15 px — the exact opposite of what catalogue DTI says (where width destroys precision: dilated-r3 catalogue DTI 0.5887 vs exact 0.9999). The metric's β-weight and 300 m kernel monetise soft, wide, structurally-anchored mass when the truth is nearby but unlabelled.

Adopted submission v1 (the file on the home page)

p(x) = max( halo(x), blind_emission(x) )
halo(x)          = 0.95 on catalogue px; 0.6·max(1 − d(x)/15, 0) otherwise
blind_emission(x)= 0.6 · clip((p_model(x) − 0.5)/0.5, 0, 1)
  • halo: recall around every mapped fault — covers splays, mis-registration, and experts' re-mapping of known systems (proxy-optimal width).
  • blind_emission: 27-feature gradient-boosted detector (19 GeoDAWN bands + 8 derived edge/texture channels, no distance-to-fault feature) — finds geophysically fault-like ground off-catalogue; bounded mass (~17k), unbounded upside if it hits private faults.

Decision rule for the selected submission

One submission counts for both rounds, chosen blind. Rule: pick the artifact with the best (proxy DTI, discovery-credibility) pair — never catalogue DTI alone. Re-evaluate after each hypothesis lands. Keep the Note field unique per artifact so the history stays auditable.
